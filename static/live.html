Before you continue

Twilio receives your phone number to send a verification SMS, then receives the code you enter to check it. The configured search provider, named on the search confirmation, receives your number only after that separate approval. The selected no-card route is Serper for Google Search results and requires active free credits. No paid top-up is authorized. Twilio is separate: its account must have sufficient approved no-card trial allowance. Do not continue if paid usage or a card is required. Provider billing is outside this app’s control.

The app keeps active phone/challenge state only in this server’s memory for 5 minutes (an already-started provider request may finish within its 5-second timeout) and clears the phone after search. It does not automatically write phone numbers, codes, results, or provider keys to files or browser storage. If you choose to download a report, it can contain personal information in listing URLs and stays on your device after you clear this session. Provider retention and your browser’s own behavior are outside ScanPlus’s control.
