CLEAR ABOUT OUR LIMITS

Useful guidance.
No impossible promises.

A public listing does not establish where spam calls come from. Removal cannot guarantee fewer calls, and information can reappear.

Which sites are included?

The demo catalog includes Spokeo, Whitepages, and TruePeopleSearch. Results are synthetic, with no real listing URLs. Official removal destinations are confirmed from the California registry; current form steps have not been independently verified.

Can ScanPlus remove every listing?

No. Future automatic requests depend on official support and your authorization. CAPTCHA, email, phone, or identity checks remain yours to complete. We do not bypass them.

What does $1.49 cover?

The intended price is $1.49 for one lookup report, with no subscription. Coverage and commercial viability are still being validated. This prototype charges nothing and accepts no payment details.

Can I use my real phone number?

Use the single number field above. A personal number takes you to an availability explanation, without sending or saving the entry. Real lookup is not connected on this site. The synthetic demo still uses +1 202-555-0147. Live lookup needs real ownership verification, approved discovery access, clear provider consent, and a privacy review.
